What this is (and isn't)

A dynasty trade advisor for Sleeper leagues. It knows every roster, every team's competitive window, who's quietly aging out, and who around the league would actually pick up the phone. Ask it anything a sharp league-mate could answer.

League is the table — click a team for its roster and a few trade ideas. Trade is the helper — pick two teams, tap pieces, see the impact. Ask is the conversation — the only part that costs anything.

  • Dynasty only. Redraft leagues get a polite no.
  • Not a trade calculator. It never adds player values into a package price — it tells you which holding beats which, who wants whom, and why an ask is hard. The negotiating is yours.
  • Projections, not results. Team reads come from roster composition, market prices and projected points. Records and playoff pace show how the season is going, but they don't decide what a roster should do.
  • Answers take a minute or two, and there's a shared daily budget — if it's spent, come back tomorrow.
